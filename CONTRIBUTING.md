# Contributing

## Editorial bar

Every entry must:

1. **Link to a primary source** — a published article, playbook, research paper, or documented case study. No blog aggregators, no summaries of summaries.
2. **Be from a named organisation** — anonymous or unattributed sources are not accepted.
3. **Be substantive** — a tweet, a LinkedIn post, or a talk abstract does not qualify. Long-form content or documented frameworks only.
4. **Not duplicate an existing entry** — if a better version of a resource already exists in the list, improve the existing entry rather than adding a new one.

## What does not get in

- Vendor marketing disguised as a playbook
- Resources behind a paywall with no public abstract or excerpt
- AI-generated content without a named human author and organisation
- Anything that cannot be independently verified

## How to submit

Open a pull request. Add your entry to the relevant section in `README.md` using this format:

```
- **[Title](url)** — Organisation, Month Year. One sentence on what it covers and why it belongs here. · [Optional second link]
```

The description is editorial: say what is useful about this resource, not just what it is.
