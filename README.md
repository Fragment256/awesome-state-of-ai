## Playbooks & Frameworks

- **[The Founder's Playbook: Building an AI-Native Startup](https://claude.com/blog/the-founders-playbook)** — Anthropic, May 2026. End-to-end practitioner guide covering persistent context, surface selection, validation discipline, measurement frameworks, security baselines, operational bottleneck mapping, and knowledge codification. · [Archived PDF](https://github.com/Fragment256/ai-native-checklist/blob/main/data/sources/founders-playbook-2026-05.pdf)

- **[The Agentic Organization](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organizational-performance/our-insights/the-agentic-organization-contours-of-the-next-paradigm-for-the-ai-era)** — McKinsey & Company, Sep 2025. Five-pillar framework (business model, operating model, governance, workforce/culture, technology/data) for redesigning an enterprise around human-agent collaboration.

- **[Software Is Changing (Again)](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/MW-andrej-karpathy-software-is-changing-again)** — Andrej Karpathy, YC AI Startup School, Jun 2025. Defines the Software 1.0/2.0/3.0 framework and what it means for how companies should build software and structure engineering teams.

## Leadership & Operating Mandates

- **[Reflexive AI Usage is Now a Baseline Expectation](https://x.com/tobi/status/1909251946235437514)** — Tobi Lütke, Shopify CEO, Apr 2025. Internal-turned-public mandate establishing AI competency as a performance requirement; teams must prove AI cannot do requested work before receiving headcount.

- **[Reflections](https://blog.samaltman.com/reflections)** — Sam Altman, OpenAI CEO, Jan 2025. Canonical statement that AI agents are materially joining the workforce as virtual co-workers in 2025 — the clearest articulation of AI-native intent from OpenAI's leadership.

## Org Design & Structure

- **[From Hierarchy to Intelligence](https://sequoiacap.com/article/from-hierarchy-to-intelligence/)** — Jack Dorsey & Roelof Botha, Sequoia Capital, Apr 2026. Corporate hierarchy as an obsolete information-routing protocol, with Block's concrete restructuring (DRIs, player-coaches, no permanent middle management, machine-readable company world model) as a live case study.

- **[The Frontier Firm is Born](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/2025-the-year-the-frontier-firm-is-born)** — Microsoft WorkLab, Apr 2025. Defines the Frontier Firm archetype — human-agent hybrid teams, on-demand intelligence — with empirical data from 31,000 workers across 31 markets on how AI-native organisations are structured and outperforming traditional firms.

## Enterprise Adoption

- **[How 100 Enterprise CIOs Are Building and Buying Gen AI](https://a16z.com/ai-enterprise-2025/)** — Andreessen Horowitz, 2025. Survey of 100 CIOs across 15 industries on AI budget growth, the shift from pilot to core operating line, and why AI-native vendors are winning enterprise deals over incumbents retrofitting AI.

- **[The State of AI in 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai)** — McKinsey QuantumBlack, Mar 2025. Annual global survey identifying what separates companies rewiring their organisation to capture AI value from those still experimenting.

## Market & Product

- **[Notes on AI Apps in 2026](https://a16z.com/notes-on-ai-apps-in-2026/)** — Andreessen Horowitz, 2026. What structural advantages AI-native products have over incumbents retrofitting AI — multi-model orchestration, cornered data, network effects, and extensive feature surface built cheaply.
